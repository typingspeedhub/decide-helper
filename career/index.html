How to use these career decision guides

Career decisions usually involve more than salary alone. A higher-paying job may require a longer commute, more stress, less flexibility, or greater risk, while a lower-paying role may offer stronger long-term opportunities. Start with the guide that matches your decision, then use a related tool when available.

  • Compare total compensation, not salary alone.
  • Consider job security, benefits, commute, and flexibility.
  • Think about skills and career growth over several years.
  • Include lifestyle, stress, and work-life balance in the decision.
  • Consider the downside if the new opportunity does not work out.
