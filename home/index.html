How to use these home decision guides

Major home decisions usually combine money with lifestyle, timing, uncertainty, and long-term commitments. A single number rarely gives the full answer. Start with the guide that matches your decision, then use the related tool when available.

  • Compare total cost rather than one monthly payment.
  • Include one-time costs, ongoing costs, and realistic downside risks.
  • Consider how long you expect the decision to affect you.
  • Keep emergency savings and financial flexibility in mind.
  • Separate personal preferences from assumptions about future prices or savings.
